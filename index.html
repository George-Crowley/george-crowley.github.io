Overview

I received my BSc in Mathematics from the University of Sussex, England in 2022, and I am currently a PhD student under the primary supervision of Dr Iñaki Esnaola at The University of Sheffield.

My research project is titled Machine Learning Methods for Monitoring of Complex Water and Sewer Network Infrastructure, sponsored by Thames Water and the EPSRC CDT in Water Infrastructure and Resilience.

Collaborators:
Dr Iñaki Esnaola Prof. Simon Tait Prof. George Panoutsos Prof. Vanessa Speight

Research Interests

My research interests lie within probability theory, information theory, numerical solution to ODEs and PDEs, and data science. My current project uses information-theoretic measures as cost functions for optimal sensor placement and machine learning for anomalous event detection in waste networks.

October 2025

Proud to share a preprint on arXiv proving and modifying concentration inequalities for scaled self-bounding functions. The results improve on existing bounds and apply to random sampling of submodular functions, e.g., mutual information.
